Privacy Policy

Maria's Assistant is a personal, home-built assistant used by one household. It is not a commercial service and has no other users.

What it accesses

With the account owner's permission, given through Google's sign-in screen, it reads Gmail messages and Google Calendar events, saves email reply drafts, sends emails the owner has approved, and adds, moves or cancels calendar events the owner has approved.

How the information is used

Only to give the account owner updates about their day, bills and appointments, to send them a summary email, and to carry out changes they confirm. Nothing is used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

Where it is stored

All processing happens on computers in the owner's home. A small database of reminders and bill amounts, and the encrypted Google sign-in token, are stored there. Nothing is sent to any other company or person, apart from Google itself.

Sharing

The information is not sold, shared or transferred to anyone.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can remove access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and can delete the stored data by deleting it from the home computer.
