Maria's Assistant

A private, home-built assistant for one household.

Maria's Assistant runs on a small computer at home. It reads one family member's Google Calendar and Gmail to give spoken and on-screen updates about the day, upcoming bills and medical checkups, and sends a daily summary email.

It is a personal project, not a product or service. It is not offered to the public, and only the household that runs it signs in to it.

Any change to the calendar or a reply to an email is made only after the account owner confirms it out loud.
